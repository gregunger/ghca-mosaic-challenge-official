06 / Security, governance & Responsible AI07:00-09:00 / 2 min

Useful autonomy.
Confident human control.

1

Use only authorized evidence

Only approved sources enter the workflow. Read-only MCP tools limit retrieval; source identity, classification, revision and integrity checks make the evidence traceable. Unapproved sources stop processing.

2

Expose uncertainty

Fact, assumption, unknown, risk, recommendation and decision labels. Facts, recommendations and decisions require valid evidence IDs. Humans assess whether evidence actually supports the claim.

3

Bound inference and cost

Approved brief and evidence; separate authenticated CLI profile; configured model, cap and deadline; no model tools, automatic paid retry or silent provider switch. Raw transcript stays outside the analysis prompt.

4

Publish only a complete revision

Sealed inputs, source/engine integrity checks, private staging, schema and authority checks, atomic publication and artifact hashes. Eligible local rebuild reuses verified analysis without another model call.

5

Protect the viewing surface

Escaped untrusted report text, blocked active Markdown content, offline report assets and heuristic content scans. Conversations and operational inputs stay in ignored local storage.

6

Keep judgment human

No source authorization, policy exception, risk acceptance, customer fact, commitment, architecture decision or release can be delegated to the generator. Qualified review is mandatory.

Two customer decisions remain unrecorded1. Authorized architecture selection→2. Approval of the exact selected dossier revision

Make governance part of the App workflow. Documented session modes, optional sandboxes and supported enterprise policies help people direct the work. MOSAIC adds explicit evidence and output checks, so teams can inspect the proposal and make informed decisions with clear accountability.

Control ownership and production prerequisites

The App and MOSAIC provide different control layers. A worktree is not tenant isolation. Prompts are not access controls. Local hashes are not signatures. A PR is not customer approval. Production identity, encryption, retention, connectors and durable execution require separate implementation and qualified review.

R07 / control gates · R08 / security boundary · R12 / blocked review record · R05 / App sessions & sandboxing. SOC 2, applicable NIST publications and ITIL 4 are customer-specific alignment targets, not certifications.

Speaker talk track / control that supports adoption

Copilot App becomes more useful when teams can direct the work and inspect the result. MOSAIC shows that value in practice: a prepared, validated proposal with a clear human-review handoff.

First, evidence access is explicit. Policies define which sources the workflow may use; the agent does not decide its own access. Source identity, classification, revision and integrity checks travel with the package. Read-only MCP tools keep evidence retrieval separate from permission to change the source. Unapproved sources stop the workflow.

Second, claims expose their status. Facts, recommendations and decisions need valid evidence references. Assumptions and unknowns stay visible. A valid citation identifier is not proof that the source supports the sentence. Qualified reviewers still check meaning.

Third, generation has a disclosed boundary. The separate analysis call uses the configured model, cap and deadline, has no tools and is not automatically retried. The raw transcript is excluded from that prompt. Complete outputs are validated before publication; an eligible recovery reuses integrity-checked analysis rather than silently buying another inference.

Finally, the customer retains two distinct decisions: architecture selection, then approval of the exact selected dossier revision. This reference records neither. A pull request supports review; it does not substitute for customer authority.

The control layers have distinct jobs: App session controls help people direct work; MOSAIC checks its evidence and outputs; qualified people own customer decisions. That gives the field a concrete governance conversation alongside the business-value conversation.

Speaker talk track / quantify without pretending

The business case for adopting the App is expert capacity. The repository's planning model uses ninety-six hours of manual intake and design preparation. Retain sixteen hours for human validation and the difference is eighty specialist hours, roughly eighty-three percent.

At an illustrative loaded value of two hundred dollars an hour, that is sixteen thousand dollars of gross capacity per eligible request. At one hundred eligible requests a year, the illustration becomes eight thousand hours and one point six million dollars before additional costs.

Those are not measured savings. They are not a promise to remove staff. They are a hypothesis about returning expert capacity to higher-value work.

This model lets us challenge the assumptions. Increase review to twenty-four hours and the gross capacity becomes seventy-two hours per request. Add correction and coordination time. Add the full cost of platforms, integration, governance and operation. If retained effort exceeds the baseline, the result turns negative; we do not hide it.

The pilot measures the whole work system: requirements coverage, corrections, expert review, total cost and productive reuse of returned time. That gives the field an App value case a business leader can inspect and test. The opportunity is more expert capacity for better customer conversations and more eligible engagements.

Key questions / scope, value and adoption

A closer look at the operating model.

Why is the App central rather than a wrapper around a script?

The authentic user journey starts with MOSAIC Orchestrator and the intake skill in GitHub Copilot App. The App carries discovery, plan review, approved tool access and the report handoff. The worker supplies bounded analysis, structural validation and publication. Its standalone CLI proves the contracts, not App use; authentic captures supply the App-use evidence. R01 R16

Is this the same as the employee policy assistant being proposed?

No. MOSAIC prepares the solution-engineering assessment. The employee assistant is the fictional solution being evaluated. Managed, custom and hybrid approaches are alternatives for that proposed solution, not three implementations MOSAIC has deployed. R10 R11

Why not Copilot Studio, Microsoft 365 Copilot or Claude Code?

Copilot App is the workbench for directing this preparation process in a GitHub-centered workflow. Copilot Studio or Microsoft 365 Copilot may be appropriate for the customer solution being proposed; the three-option analysis preserves that choice. Our App preference is native GitHub workflow fit and reusable field practice. Claude Code has overlapping tools and workflow capabilities; no comparative speed, cost or quality advantage has been measured. R10 R22

Can any customer, in any industry, use it immediately?

That is an adoption ambition, not established fit or a current license grant. Shared preparation needs make reuse plausible, but each customer needs authorized evidence, industry-specific controls, identity, connectors, review policy, operating ownership and evaluation. A second isolated pattern and approved redistribution rights remain gates. R14 R20

No measured outcome supports that conclusion. No such claim is established. The current runtime assumes a trusted local user and synthetic data. Not by default. Customer policy must authorize the architecture selection first, and later approval must bind the exact selected dossier revision in the customer's workflow.
