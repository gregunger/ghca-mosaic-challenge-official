MOSAIC—Multi-customer Orchestration System for Adaptive Intake and Continuous Delivery—is AI-first business process automation for solution engineering. A business user describes a problem in plain language; adaptive intake asks relevant questions, approved evidence grounds the analysis, and an eight-stage governed process produces a 23-file solution dossier. It covers business context, scope, requirements, assumptions, unknowns, risks, three architecture options, proposed design, delivery-readiness plans, validation, audit and meeting materials. Small businesses through large enterprises can adopt the reusable process with isolated policies, evidence, customizable reports and approvals. People still select the architecture, approve the exact revision and authorize delivery. The illustrative case reduces 96 preparation hours to 16 retained review hours: 80 hours returned, about 83%, or $16,000 gross capacity at $200/hour before incremental costs. These are assumptions, not measured savings. This creates a simpler, faster, more consistent and traceable path from business need to human decision.
