Organizations lose time, effort and money when solution engineering is fragmented across vague requests, scattered research, disconnected documents and reconstructed handoffs. MOSAIC's GitHub Copilot App edition is AI-first business process automation for that lifecycle: adaptive intake, approved evidence, structured analysis, exactly three options, proposed design, delivery-readiness planning, deterministic validation and a 23-artifact dossier for accountable human decisions. Its proposed advantage is reusable Microsoft field practice maintained beside GitHub issues, instructions, diffs and review history, not an exclusive model capability. Small businesses through large enterprises can adopt the process with isolated customer policies, evidence and approvals. The illustrative case returns 80 hours and $16,000 gross capacity per request at $200/hour, assuming 96 manual hours and 16 retained review hours; added effort and costs reduce the benefit. Claude Code also supports instructions, tools, worktrees and GitHub integration, so similar workflows are possible there and no head-to-head advantage is claimed. Authentic App source-tool use and repeatable 23-artifact packages are demonstrated; full conversational App execution and specialist handoffs remain uncaptured. Effort, quality, lifecycle time and total cost still require comparative evaluation.
