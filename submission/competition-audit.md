## Assessment

The original pitch under-explained the architect's problem and over-emphasized stages, hashes, and feature counts. It also blurred completed local proof with planned hosted workflow and customer portability. Those were substantive weaknesses, not a lack of decorative polish.

MOSAIC means **Multi-customer Orchestration System for Adaptive Intake and Continuous Delivery**. It is provider-neutral, AI-first business process automation and orchestration for solution engineering, designed for adoption from small businesses through large enterprises. The product automates the repeatable path from plain-language business need through adaptive intake, approved evidence, normalization, analysis, three options, proposed design, delivery-readiness planning, validation and accountable human review. The 23-artifact dossier is the durable process record, not the full product definition. This Copilot App contest reference is a subset that stops before architecture selection, implementation, deployment and release. The Microsoft opportunity is reusable expertise, increased field capacity, lower avoidable cost, less duplicated work, simpler process, stronger reporting consistency, preserved knowledge and a shorter path from intent to delivery. Executing prepared implementation, verification, security, deployment/rollback and operations/adoption plans remains a separately authorized [extension](../docs/adoption-playbook.md#governed-sdlc-extension).

The economic opportunity is now quantified as a planning case: 96 manual intake/design hours minus 16 retained review hours returns 80 specialist hours, about 83%, worth $16,000 gross capacity at an assumed $200/hour. At an illustrative 100 eligible requests annually, that models 8,000 hours and $1.6 million before additional costs. These are not measured results, cash savings, customer-volume forecasts or staffing commitments. The [economic model](../docs/evaluation-plan.md#planning-economics) includes provenance, review sensitivity, volume illustrations and cost thresholds. SOC 2, NIST, and ITIL 4 support scoped customer-specific alignment, not an unearned compliance claim. Universal fit, superiority to human engineers, and superiority to Microsoft's entire portfolio are not established. Strong positioning does not require inventing those results and cannot guarantee a competition outcome.

Current status: the technical workflow through integrity-bound retained-response recovery is complete locally, and the owner released the media hold after accepting the workflow quality on September 29. Bonus feedback remains selected. The 148-word summary and three-slide deck define MOSAIC as AI-first business process automation for solution engineering and explain the fragmented-process problem, automated lifecycle, customer-specific reporting, enterprise adoption range, human-review stop and illustrative value case. The authentic 2:55 App workflow video is published at `https://youtu.be/RP4CeCYt4VE` and proves intake through browser handoff. The target is all 100 base points plus the five-point bonus opportunity. No customer solution was implemented, deployed or approved.
